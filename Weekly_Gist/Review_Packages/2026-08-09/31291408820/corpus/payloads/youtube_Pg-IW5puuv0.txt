Most people think open models are the cheap option, what you use when you can't afford the big labs. But they've gotten so good that they can now handle about 90% of what most people do, and you can run a solid AI agent at home on a $600 graphics card.

Zach Mueller is Head of Developer Relations at Lambda and has been living in open source inference for over a decade, starting at Hugging Face. In this session he breaks down how to actually think about open models: which ones matter right now, the napkin math for running them at home, the quantization and tokens/second trade offs that decide your setup, the security case for hosting yourself over routing through a third party, and how to slot small local models into Claude Code and Codex to cut your token bill.
