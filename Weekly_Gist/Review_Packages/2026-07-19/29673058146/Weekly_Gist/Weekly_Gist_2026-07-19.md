*   **Yukuan Lu, Zaishuo Xia, Weyl Lu, Yubei Chen** — Concept-Guided Spatial Regularization for World Models in Atari Pong (Paper) — 2026-07-16 — [https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15142](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15142)
    *   **TL;DR:** World models in Atari Pong often fail to properly model critical concepts like the ball, and adding a loss that focuses on segmented key concepts improves rollouts and policy learning.
    *   **Takeaways:** Across five visual world-model agents in Pong, frozen world models exhibit failures like ball disappearance and incorrect dynamics. Zero-shot MBRL using frozen world models yields much weaker policies than original training pipelines. Insufficient modeling of task-critical concepts (e.g., the ball) is a key bottleneck. Concept-Guided Spatial Regularization (CGSReg) adds an auxiliary loss on segmented regions, improving model rollouts and policy performance. CGSReg helps but doesn't fix all world-model issues; other bottlenecks remain.
    *   **Implication for Rex Ren:** This paper underscores that even in simple simulated environments, world models can fail to capture the essential structure of reality, suggesting that formalizing what matters (concepts) is crucial for agents to effectively manipulate their world.

*   **Susie Lu, Haonan Chen, Weirui Ye, Yilun Du** — DriftWorld: Fast World Modeling through Drifting (Paper) — 2026-07-16 — [https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15065](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15065)
    *   **TL;DR:** DriftWorld is a fast world model for robotic agents that generates future frames in a single forward pass using drifting generative models, enabling efficient planning at 30+ fps.
    *   **Takeaways:** DriftWorld replaces iterative denoising with a learned action-conditioned drift, achieving 17x faster rollouts than diffusion-based world models. It attains state-of-the-art decision-making on multiple robot manipulation benchmarks with significantly lower inference time. The model can serve as an offline simulator for ranking real robot policies, with rollout-based scores correlating with ground truth up to 0.99. The approach shows that drifting generative models are well-suited for world modeling where fast, high-quality imagination is crucial for planning and policy evaluation.
    *   **Implication for Rex Ren:** By drastically accelerating world model rollouts, DriftWorld pushes agentic systems closer to real-time, flexible imagination—a building block for agents that seamlessly blend simulated and physical realities.
