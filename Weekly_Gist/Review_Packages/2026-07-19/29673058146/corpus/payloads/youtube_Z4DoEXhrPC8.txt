Jeson Patel, CTO at 11X, explains how a bug triaging process that once ran on one PM's personal machine turned into a Slack-native AI agent used across the company. He walks through why he chose LangSmith Fleet to build without writing infrastructure code, how the agent expanded to cover Datadog alert triage and general Q&amp;A, and why he now wants every team member building their own agents.
