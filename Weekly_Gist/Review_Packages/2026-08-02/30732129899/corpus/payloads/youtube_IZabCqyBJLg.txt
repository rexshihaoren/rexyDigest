Robert Xu from the LangChain team breaks down what Deep Agents actually are, how the "harness" concept fits into the model plus harness equation, and how Deep Agents relate to LangChain and LangGraph. 

The second half covers what changes when you move a deep agent into production, including durable execution, short and long term memory, authentication, and human in the loop design. Recorded at a LangChain meetup.
