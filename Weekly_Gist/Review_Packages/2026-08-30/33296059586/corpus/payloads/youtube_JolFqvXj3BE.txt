You get a coding agent working on your laptop, then you want ten of them running at once. The thing that breaks is never the agent, It is the machine you handed it, and most teams spend months rebuilding that machine badly before they realize the environment was the actual product.

Adam Azzam works at Modal and was previously a maintainer of Prefect and FastMCP. He has watched dozens of companies build their own background agents, and they are all landing on the same system architecture. In this talk he explains why a bare sandbox is not enough, why treating agent environments like CI is what gives you three minute cold starts, how Ramp runs close to a million sandboxes with sub second boots, and where the agent itself should actually live. The last section is code, building this on Modal.
