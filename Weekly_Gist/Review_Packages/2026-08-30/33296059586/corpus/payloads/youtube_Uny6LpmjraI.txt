Jeff Barg (Head of AI), Vyshu Khota (ML Engineer), and Soroush Khadem (Software Engineer) at Clay break down how they built and scaled Claygent and Sculptor, Clay's go to market research and engineering agents. They cover the eval philosophy that keeps agentic changes safe to ship, why they moved to a data lake architecture to give agents first class access to Clay's data, and how a large context breakthrough from Fable changed what their agents can do with production data.
