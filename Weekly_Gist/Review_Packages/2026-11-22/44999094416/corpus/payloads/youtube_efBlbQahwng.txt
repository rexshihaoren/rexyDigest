Jeff Barg is Head of AI at Clay, the go-to-market platform for list building, data enrichment and orchestration, with a dataset of 900 million contacts and 40 million companies. Clay recently launched a new global agent, built around a coding agent that runs on the same CLI external agents use. In this conversation, Jeff explains why Clay got more out of harness engineering than post-training, how the agent asks for approval on 100+ line Bash commands, and why he’d rather charge for tokens than outcomes.

We also discuss:
• Where a first-party agent beats a plugin on latency and cost
• Making a SQL-like query language for Clay’s search platform
• The case for picking an off-the-shelf harness
• Why Codex is “really bad” at debugging long traces
• Why memory came before the agent
