AI agent to research something and it fires off a dozen very specific questions. Most search systems answer all twelve with the same handful of documents. That is not a bug in anyone's implementation, and there is a proof you cannot scale your way out of it.

Marek is the CEO and co-founder of TopK, before that he led the data plane at Pinecone for three years. In this talk he makes the case for late interaction, which keeps a representation for every token instead of squashing a document into one summary vector. Marek walks through the algorithm his team built to make that affordable, and what it looks like at billion document scale.
