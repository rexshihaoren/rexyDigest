Brendan McManus, Michael Ran, and Santi Weight from Bridgewater Associates' applied AI team walk through PAT, the Pocket Analyst Tool — an internal AI analyst deployed to hundreds of investors that performs hours of deep exploratory research in minutes. Brendan explains how 50 years of written-down investment logic gave Bridgewater a unique advantage entering the AI era. Michael demos PAT live — covering time series search with human-like inspection (jumping from 50% to 90% accuracy), per-user security harnesses, and a Teach button that autonomously generates benchmarks and PRs to fix its own mistakes. Santi then goes deep on the architecture: treating agentic code generation as a compiler problem, parallel LLM code gen from a structured plan, DAG-based validation agents, and a static analysis caching layer that makes iterative re-runs nearly instantaneous.
