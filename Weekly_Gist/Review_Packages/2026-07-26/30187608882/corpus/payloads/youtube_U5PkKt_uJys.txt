LangChain Co-Founder and CEO Harrison Chase sits down with Traversal co-founders Anish Agarwal and Raj Agrawal to talk about building an AI SRE. 

They cover the jump from causal machine learning research at MIT to troubleshooting petabyte-scale production incidents, how Traversal's agent harness handles search, memory, and the production world model, and where DIY agent building tends to break down. The talk closes with an audience Q&amp;A on swarms, sub agents, and evals.
